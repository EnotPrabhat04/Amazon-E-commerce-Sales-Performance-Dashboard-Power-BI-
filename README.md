# Amazon-E-commerce-Sales-Performance-Dashboard-Power-BI-
This project features an interactive Executive Dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI to provide a comprehensive, top-level view of key business performance indicators (KPIs) related to sales, revenue, and customer engagement.

**Project Overview & Key Features**
**Objective:** To transform raw sales and order data into actionable insights, enabling executives to monitor performance, track goals, and identify regional trends at a glance.

**Technology Stack:**

Data Visualization Tool: Microsoft Power BI Desktop

Data Source: Excel

Data Modeling/Preparation: Power Query (M language) and DAX (Data Analysis eXpressions)

Dashboard Focus: The dashboard consolidates critical metrics on a single, clean page, focusing on Monthly Revenue, Order Quantity, Total Profit, and Customer Growth.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Captured**
The dashboard effectively tracks and visualizes several core business metrics:

**Financial Metrics:**

Total Revenue: **$7.4M**

Total Profit:** $3.1M**

Revenue Return %:** 14.6%**

Target Achievement: A central card highlights the week's profit, showing $49,127.1 and a target achievement of +19.38%.

Operational Metrics:

Order Quantity: **12.4K**

**Customer & Trend Metrics:**

**Total Customers:** A line graph visualizes the trend of Total Customers by Start of Week, indicating a steady growth over time.

**Top Country by Orders:** Highlighted as Australia, indicating a key market for the business.

**Visualizations & Analysis**
The dashboard uses a variety of effective visuals to convey data:

**Card Visuals: **Used for quick, high-impact visibility of major KPIs ($7.4M Revenue, $3.1M Profit).

**Line/Bar Charts:** Used to track Monthly Revenue and Total Customers by Start of Week trends over time.

**Table/Matrix Visual:** A detailed view is included showing Product Names (e.g., Mountain-700, Road-47) with corresponding Order Quantity, Total Revenue, and Total Profit.

**Geographic Map:** Displays Total Revenue by Country, with interactive slicers to filter data by Continent (e.g., Europe, North America, Pacific).

This project demonstrates strong proficiency in data modeling, DAX calculation creation, and the design of user-friendly, high-level business intelligence solutions using Power BI.
